How we got here.

  1. Thailand

    We met while studying abroad. Two travelers, which explains a lot about everything that came after.

  2. Meta

    We both spent years at Meta teaching brands how to market. Mariah was at Amazon too.

  3. Bamboo Ave

    Then we left to build something of our own. Bamboo Ave came first, clothes made for how we actually live and travel.

  4. Bali

    We moved our life and our companies to Bali. No office, no commute.

  5. AI

    Tray rebuilt how our companies run around a team of AI agents. Mariah started teaching people to do the same, and Avori is how we're bringing that way of working to every team.
